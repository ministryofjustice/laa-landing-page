Sign in to Legal Aid Services

Sign in to Legal Aid Services (SiLAS) is a secure gateway to view and access all the Legal Aid Services required to carry out your role.

If you have not used SiLAS before, first you need to Activate Your Account

Everyone benefits from the secure sign-in technology provided by SiLAS. Some users with additional responsibilities can use SiLAS for other tasks.

If you manage the users in your firm, you can use SiLAS to:

  • View and manage the users linked to your firm
  • Assign services, roles and offices to users
  • Delegate access to users outside your firm (for example, users who work on behalf of multiple firms)

If you work on behalf of multiple firms, you can use SiLAS to:

  • View the firms you have been given access to
  • Switch between different firms
